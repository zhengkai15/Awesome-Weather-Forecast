# 🤖 LLM, Agent & Multimodal Weather

Papers at the intersection of large language models, multimodal learning, and AI agents applied to weather and climate science.

---

## 🗣️ LLM for Weather Understanding

Using LLMs to interpret, explain, and narrate meteorological information.

- **[ClimateChat]** (ICLR 2025) — *Instruction-tuned dialogue LLM specialized for climate science question answering.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09646)
  - 👥 Taoning Wang et al.

- **[GPT-4V Meteorology]** (arXiv 2024.04) — *Systematic evaluation of GPT-4V's ability to interpret meteorological charts, satellite imagery, and weather maps.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12452)

- **[Exploring LLMs for Climate Forecasting]** (arXiv 2024.11) — *Explores the capability and limitations of GPT-4o and other LLMs for direct rainfall prediction.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18834)

- **[WeatherSyn]** (ICML 2026) — *Multimodal LLM automatically generating human-readable weather forecast reports from numerical gridded fields.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07787)

- **[VLM for Textual Weather Forecasts]** (arXiv 2024.12) — *VLM converting numerical gridded forecast fields directly into structured textual weather forecasts.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10450)

- **[AI-Meteorologist]** (arXiv 2024.12) — *LLM Agent fusing multi-source meteorological data to automatically generate narrative weather reports.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10950)

## 🔗 LLM + Forecast Integration

Combining LLMs with numerical or AI-based forecasting pipelines.

- **[ClimateLLM]** (arXiv 2025.02) — *Frequency-aware Mixture-of-Experts LLM forecasting framework mapping meteorological time series to token sequences.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06202)

- **[CLLMate]** (EMNLP 2025) — *Multimodal alignment of ERA5 gridded reanalysis data with climate event news text for joint forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09744)

- **[Weather-R1]** (ICASSP 2026) — *Reinforcement learning fine-tuning of a multimodal VLM for logically consistent meteorological reasoning and forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12523)

- **[Hierarchical AI-Meteorologist]** (arXiv 2025.11) — *Hierarchical LLM-Agent pipeline: retrieval → reasoning → generation of complete meteorological forecast reports.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.06474)

## 🌐 Multimodal Weather Models

Joint modeling of imagery, text, and numerical meteorological data.

- **[Omni-Weather]** (arXiv 2025) — *First multimodal foundation model unifying radar weather generation and understanding, incorporating Chain-of-Thought reasoning.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21643)
  - 👥 Chenghao Jiang et al.

- **[ClimateIQA]** (arXiv 2024.06) — *Meteorological heatmap VQA dataset with SPOT localization algorithm, benchmarking VLM spatial understanding of weather fields.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10094)

- **[WeatherQA]** (arXiv 2024.06) — *Multimodal dataset for causal reasoning about severe weather events, with image-text QA pairs sourced from NOAA official reports.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11217) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/yuchenS-16/WeatherQA)

- **[CLLMate Dataset]** (EMNLP 2025) — *Multimodal climate event forecasting benchmark pairing ERA5 time-series with climate event news text.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09744)

## 🤖 Agent-Driven Weather Science

LLM agents autonomously orchestrating tools, models, and data for forecasting and scientific discovery.

- **[Zephyrus]** (arXiv 2025.10) — *Tool-calling meteorological science agent with ZephyrusBench evaluation suite, autonomously querying numerical models and observational databases.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07287)

- **[AgentCaster]** (arXiv 2025.10) — *Multimodal LLM Agent for high-impact tornado forecasting, fusing radar imagery with textual surface observations.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02480)

- **[ClimateAgent]** (arXiv 2025.11) — *Multi-agent orchestration framework automating the full pipeline of climate data retrieval, analysis, and visualization.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16497)

## 📏 Benchmarks & Datasets

Evaluation frameworks and datasets for assessing LLM / multimodal capabilities in weather and climate.

- **[WeatherBench 2]** (JAMES 2024) — *Standardized global medium-range forecast evaluation framework for fair comparison of AI models against ECMWF.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15560) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/google-research/weatherbench2)
  - 👥 Stephan Rasp et al. (Google)

- **[ClimSim]** (NeurIPS 2023 Best Paper) — *5.7-billion-sample climate physics simulation dataset for ML-based parameterization replacing traditional numerical schemes.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08754) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/leap-stc/ClimSim)
  - 👥 Sungduk Yu et al.

- **[ClimaQA]** (ICLR 2025) — *Semi-automated evaluation framework for climate-domain LLM question answering with scientific accuracy scoring.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16701)

- **[WXImpactBench]** (ACL 2025) — *Benchmark evaluating LLM capability to predict societal impacts of meteorological disasters.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12097)

- **[MSEarth]** (ACL 2026) — *Multi-scale Earth science multimodal QA benchmark covering meteorology, oceanography, and land surface tasks.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16573)
