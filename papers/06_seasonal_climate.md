# 🌡️ Seasonal & Climate Forecast

Sub-seasonal to seasonal (S2S) prediction and long-range climate simulation using ML.

---

## 🆕 2024–2026

- **[NeuralGCM]** (Nature 2024) — *Hybrid GCM enabling decade-scale climate simulation and realistic tropical cyclone statistics while retaining medium-range skill.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07222) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/neuralgcm/neuralgcm)
  - 👥 Dmitrii Kochkov et al. (Google)

- **[ENSO-PhyNet]** (npj Climate & Atmos. Sci. 2024) — *Physics-informed Transformer incorporating heat budget dynamics, extending Niño 3.4 skill to 22 months.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41612-024-00741-y)
  - 👥 Zijie Guo et al.

- **[S2S DL Optimization]** (arXiv 2024) — *Multi-stage deep learning optimization strategy significantly improving S2S skill, exceeding ECMWF-S2S by 19–91%.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16728)
  - 👥 Jing-Jia Luo et al.

- **[SEEDS]** (Science Advances 2024) — *Diffusion model rapidly generating GEFS ensemble emulations 1000× faster than NWP, supporting seasonal probabilistic forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14066)
  - 👥 Lizao Li et al. (Google)

## 📚 Foundational Work

- **[FuXi-S2S]** (Nature Communications 2024) — *ML model for global 42-day daily forecasts, extending MJO skill from 30 to 36 days.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09926)
  - 👥 Xiaohui Zhong et al.

- **[ENSO-DL]** (Nature 2019) — *Deep learning for ENSO prediction demonstrating 18-month lead time skill.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1559-7)
  - 👥 Yoo-Geun Ham et al.

- **[Seasonal-Precip]** (Communications Earth & Environment 2021) — *Training ML models on climate model output for skillful interpretable seasonal precipitation forecasts.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-021-00225-4)

- **[Climate-DL]** (arXiv 2023) — *Climate model-driven seasonal forecasting approach with deep learning.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10480)
