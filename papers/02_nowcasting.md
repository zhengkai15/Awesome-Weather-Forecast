# ⚡ NowCasting (0–6 Hours)

Short-range forecasting focusing on precipitation, radar echo extrapolation, and convective events.

---

## 🌧️ Precipitation

- **[NowcastNet]** (Nature 2023) — *Physics-constrained generative adversarial network for skilful 8-hour extreme precipitation nowcasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06184-4)
  - 👥 Yuchen Zhang et al.

- **[Corrformer]** (Nature Machine Intelligence 2023) — *Interpretable weather forecasting for worldwide stations with a unified deep model.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-023-00667-9)
  - 👥 Haixu Wu et al.

- **[SRNDiff]** (arXiv 2024) — *Short-term rainfall nowcasting with conditional diffusion model.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13737)
  - 👥 Jiayi Shen et al.

- **[MetNet-3]** (arXiv 2023) — *Deep learning for day-ahead forecasts from sparse observations at 1-km, 2-minute resolution.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06079)
  - 👥 Lasse Espeholt et al. (Google)

- **[MetNet-2]** (Nature Communications 2022) — *Deep learning for 12-hour precipitation forecasts.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-32483-x)
  - 👥 Lasse Espeholt et al. (Google)

- **[MetNet]** (arXiv 2020) — *Neural weather model for precipitation forecasting up to 8 hours.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.12140)
  - 👥 Casper Kaae Sønderby et al. (Google)

- **[PreDiff]** (NeurIPS 2023) — *Latent diffusion model for precipitation nowcasting with physics-informed knowledge-guided sampling.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.10422)
  - 👥 Zhihan Gao et al.

- **[DGMR]** (arXiv 2021) — *Deep generative model for probabilistic precipitation nowcasting with accurate uncertainty quantification.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00954)
  - 👥 Suman Ravuri et al. (Google DeepMind)

- **[SwinRDM]** (AAAI 2023) — *Integrating SwinRNN with diffusion model for high-resolution and high-quality weather forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25105)
  - 👥 Cong Wang et al.

- **[AlphaPre]** (CVPR 2025) — *Amplitude-phase disentanglement model for precipitation nowcasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Lin_AlphaPre_Amplitude-Phase_Disentanglement_Model_for_Precipitation_Nowcasting_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/linkenghong/AlphaPre)
  - 👥 Kenghong Lin et al.

- **[DiffCast]** (CVPR 2024) — *Unified diffusion framework decomposing precipitation into global deterministic motion and local stochastic residuals.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06734) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/DeminYu98/DiffCast)
  - 👥 Demin Yu et al.

- **[CasCast]** (ICML 2024) — *Cascaded framework decoupling mesoscale distribution and small-scale stochasticity for high-resolution extreme precipitation.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04290) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/OpenEarthLab/CasCast)
  - 👥 Junchao Gong et al.

- **[WoFSCast]** (GRL 2025) — *First GraphCast-based thunderstorm-warning-scale ML model for 2-hour ensemble convective forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2024GL112383)
  - 👥 David John Gagne II et al. (NCAR)

- **[StormCast]** (Science Advances 2026) — *Generative diffusion model emulating NOAA HRRR 3 km convection-allowing model for 1–6 hour km-scale convective forecasts.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10958)
  - 👥 Lilli Freischem et al.

## 📡 Radar Echo Extrapolation

- **[GAN-Radar]** (Remote Sensing, MDPI 2022) — *Realistic and detailed deep-learning-based radar echo extrapolation via GAN.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/14/1/24)
  - 👥 Junchao Gong et al.

- **[ω-GNN]** (GRL 2023) — *Graph neural network coupling physical factors for precipitation forecast in China.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2023GL106676)
  - 👥 Hao Peng et al.

- **[MultiScaleGAN]** (IEEE TGRS 2022) — *Generative adversarial network experimental study for precipitation nowcasting at multiple scales.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9780397) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/luochuyao/MultiScaleGAN)
  - 👥 Luochao Yao et al.
