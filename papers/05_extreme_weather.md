# 🌪️ Extreme Weather (including Typhoon / Tropical Cyclone)

ML approaches for predicting, simulating, and understanding high-impact weather events.

---

## ⛈️ General Extreme Weather

- **[FuXi-Extreme]** (arXiv 2023) — *FuXi branch optimized for extreme rainfall and wind forecasts using diffusion model post-processing.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19822)
  - 👥 Xiaohui Zhong et al.

- **[ExtremeCast]** (arXiv 2024) — *Asymmetric loss function (Exloss) highlighting extreme value optimization for improved global medium-range extreme weather prediction.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01295)
  - 👥 Wentao Hu et al.

- **[WoFSCast]** (GRL 2025) — *First GraphCast-based storm-warning-scale ML model for Watch-to-Warning convective guidance.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2024GL112383)
  - 👥 David John Gagne II et al. (NCAR)

- **[StormCast]** (Science Advances 2026) — *Generative diffusion km-scale convection simulation with physically realistic cold pools and mesoscale convective systems.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10958)
  - 👥 Lilli Freischem et al.

## 🌀 Typhoon / Tropical Cyclone

- **[MSCAR]** (arXiv 2024) — *Multi-modal multi-scale causal autoregressive model for global tropical cyclone intensity forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13270)
  - 👥 Cong Bai et al.

- **[AIWP TC Evaluation]** (arXiv 2024) — *Systematic evaluation of multiple AI weather models for tropical cyclone track and intensity forecasting, revealing limitations of AI intensity prediction.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06735)
  - 👥 Tao Lian et al.

- **[3DTCR]** (arXiv 2026.03) — *Physics-based conditional Flow Matching framework for vortex-following 3D TC structure reconstruction, reducing WS10M RMSE by 36.5% over FuXi and surpassing ECMWF-HRES at nearly all lead times up to 5 days.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13049)
  - 👥 Hao Li et al. (Fudan University / SIAIS)
