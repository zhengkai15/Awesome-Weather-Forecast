# 🔄 Data Assimilation

ML-based approaches for combining observations with model backgrounds to produce accurate initial conditions.

---

## 🆕 2024–2026

- **[DiffDA]** (ICML 2024) — *Diffusion model using pre-trained GraphCast as backbone for recovering 0.25° global atmospheric initial fields from sparse observations.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05932) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/spcl/DiffDA)
  - 👥 Langwen Huang et al.

- **[LDA (Latent DA)]** (Science Advances 2026) — *Bayesian variational assimilation in Swin-Transformer latent space, achieving physically consistent global atmospheric DA.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02884)
  - 👥 Hang Fan et al.

- **[Neural Incremental DA]** (arXiv 2024) — *Coarse-to-fine Gaussian prior sequential neural network assimilation, end-to-end trainable for diverse observation operators.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15076)
  - 👥 Shengyu Chen et al.

- **[FuXi-DA]** (arXiv 2024) — *Generalized deep learning data assimilation framework for assimilating satellite observations.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08522)
  - 👥 Xiaohui Zhong et al.
