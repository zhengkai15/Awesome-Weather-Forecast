# 🔬 Super Resolution & Downscaling

Statistical and dynamical downscaling from coarse-resolution NWP/climate model outputs to high-resolution fields.

---

## 🆕 2024–2026

- **[STVD]** (NeurIPS 2024) — *Spatiotemporal video diffusion model for precipitation super-resolution, achieving new SOTA over California and Himalayas.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06071)
  - 👥 Noal Yossef et al.

- **[Generative Diffusion Downscaling]** (arXiv 2024) — *Generative diffusion approach for climate downscaling, restoring 0.25° ERA5 from 2° inputs.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17752)
  - 👥 Guannan Hu et al.

- **[StormCast]** (Science Advances 2026) — *Generative diffusion emulating 3 km HRRR, enabling global coarse-to-km-scale dynamical downscaling replacement.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10958)
  - 👥 Lilli Freischem et al.

- **[CorrDiff / Corr-Diff]** (arXiv 2023) — *NVIDIA residual corrective diffusion model for km-scale atmospheric downscaling (25km→2km, Taiwan).*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15214)
  - 👥 Morteza Mardani et al. (NVIDIA)

- **[FengWu-GHR]** (arXiv 2024) — *Learning kilometer-scale medium-range global weather forecasting via high-resolution latent representation.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00059)
  - 👥 Hang Fan et al.

## 📚 Classical & Prior Work

- **[Uformer-Downscaling]** (QJRMS 2023) — *Transformer-based spatial downscaling and bias correction of near-surface temperature and wind-speed forecasts.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://rmets.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/qj.4596)

- **[RS-SR Survey]** (Earth-Science Reviews 2023) — *Comprehensive review of deep learning-based remote sensing image super-resolution methods.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012825222001945)

- **[RCMs-Emulator]** (Climate Dynamics 2022) — *Regional climate model emulator based on deep learning: concept and first evaluation of a novel hybrid downscaling approach.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00382-022-06343-9)

- **[Wind-ESRGAN]** (NeurIPS 2021 Climate Workshop) — *Comparative study of CNN models for wind field downscaling via super-resolution GAN.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.climatechange.ai/papers/neurips2021/17)

- **[SRDRN]** (Water Resources Research 2021) — *Deep learning for daily precipitation and temperature downscaling.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2020WR029308)

- **[PSD-Net]** (IEEE TGRS 2021) — *Stochastic super-resolution for downscaling time-evolving atmospheric fields with a generative adversarial network.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9246532)

- **[DeepRU]** (Meteorological Applications 2020) — *Comparative study of CNN models for wind field downscaling.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://rmets.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/met.1961)

- **[YNet]** (KDD 2020) — *Climate downscaling using YNet: deep convolutional network with skip connections and fusion.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3394486.3403366)

- **[CDN]** (Mathematical Problems in Engineering 2020) — *Climate downscaling deep learning model considering multiscale spatial correlations and chaos of meteorological events.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.hindawi.com/journals/mpe/2020/7897824/)

- **[JAMC-Downscaling-I]** (J. Applied Meteorology & Climatology 2020) — *Deep-learning-based gridded downscaling in complex terrain. Part I: daily maximum and minimum 2-m temperature.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/apme/59/12/jamc-d-20-0057.1.xml)

- **[JAMC-Downscaling-II]** (J. Applied Meteorology & Climatology 2020) — *Deep-learning-based gridded downscaling in complex terrain. Part II: daily precipitation.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/apme/59/12/jamc-d-20-0058.1.xml)

- **[PSD-Net-NWP]** (NeurIPS 2019 ML4PS) — *Numerical weather model super-resolution via deep learning.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://ml4physicalsciences.github.io/2019/files/NeurIPS_ML4PS_2019_75.pdf)

- **[ML-SD-Review]** (Theoretical and Applied Climatology 2019) — *Intercomparison of machine learning methods for statistical downscaling: the case of daily and extreme precipitation.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00704-018-2613-3)

- **[DeepSD]** (KDD 2017) — *Generating high-resolution climate change projections through single image super-resolution.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3097983.3098004)
