# 🗜️ Weather Data Compression

Compact latent representations for large-scale atmospheric datasets (ERA5, CMIP6, etc.).

---

- **[CRA5]** (arXiv 2024) — *VAEformer compressing 226 TB ERA5 to 0.7 TB (>300:1 ratio) while preserving downstream forecast accuracy.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03376)
  - 👥 Tao Han et al.

- **[FuXi-Regional]** (arXiv 2024) — *Compressing high-resolution data through latent representation encoding for downscaling large-scale AI weather forecast models.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09109)
  - 👥 Xiaohui Zhong et al.

- **[WLA (Weather Latent Autoencoder)]** (arXiv 2025) — *Encoding the complete ERA5 dataset (244 TB) into a 0.43 TB latent dataset, enabling efficient downstream task training.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06623)
  - 👥 Zhengyi Luo et al.
