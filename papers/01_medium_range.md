# 🌍 Medium-Range Weather Forecast (1–15 Days)

Global and regional deterministic / ensemble forecasts at 1–15 day lead times.

---

- **[Pangu-Weather]** (Nature 2023) — *3D Earth-specific transformer achieving accurate medium-range global weather forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06185-3)
  - 👥 Kaifeng Bi et al.

- **[GraphCast]** (Science 2023) — *GNN-based model for skillful medium-range global weather forecasting, outperforming ECMWF HRES.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adi2336)
  - 👥 Remi Lam et al. (Google DeepMind)

- **[FuXi]** (npj Climate & Atmos. Sci. 2023) — *Cascade ML forecasting system for 15-day global weather forecast.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41612-023-00512-1)
  - 👥 Lei Chen et al.

- **[FengWu]** (arXiv 2023) — *Pushing skillful global medium-range weather forecast beyond 10 days lead.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02948)
  - 👥 Kang Chen et al.

- **[ClimaX]** (ICML 2023) — *First foundation model for weather and climate, pre-trained on heterogeneous climate datasets.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10343)
  - 👥 Tung Nguyen et al. (Microsoft Research)

- **[W-MAE]** (arXiv 2023) — *Pre-trained weather model with masked autoencoder for multi-variable weather forecasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08754)
  - 👥 Tao Chen et al.

- **[SFNO / SFNO]** (arXiv 2023) — *Spherical Fourier Neural Operators learning stable dynamics on the sphere.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03838)
  - 👥 Boris Bonev et al. (NVIDIA)

- **[FourCastNet]** (arXiv 2022) — *Global data-driven high-resolution weather model using Adaptive Fourier Neural Operators.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11214)
  - 👥 Jaideep Pathak et al. (NVIDIA)

- **[Aurora]** (Nature 2025) — *1.3B-parameter atmospheric foundation model surpassing specialized models across forecasting, air quality, and extreme weather tasks.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13063)
  - 👥 Cristian Bodnar et al. (Microsoft Research)

- **[GenCast]** (Nature 2024) — *Diffusion-based probabilistic ensemble forecasting outperforming ECMWF ENS on 97.2% of metrics.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15796)
  - 👥 Ilan Price et al. (Google DeepMind)

- **[Stormer]** (NeurIPS 2024) — *Minimalist transformer with randomized dynamics objectives, outperforming prior methods at 7+ day lead times.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03876) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/tung-nd/stormer)
  - 👥 Tung Nguyen et al.

- **[AIFS]** (ECMWF Tech. Memo. 2024) — *ECMWF's first operational GNN data-driven system replacing IFS deterministic forecast in production.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01465)
  - 👥 Simon Lang et al. (ECMWF)

- **[AIFS-CRPS]** (arXiv 2024) — *CRPS-trained ensemble ML model, outperforming IFS ensemble median on 97%+ metrics.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15832)
  - 👥 Simon Lang et al. (ECMWF)

- **[NeuralGCM]** (Nature 2024) — *Differentiable atmospheric dynamical solver + ML parameterization, the first hybrid GCM unifying weather and climate simulation.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07222) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/neuralgcm/neuralgcm)
  - 👥 Dmitrii Kochkov et al. (Google)

- **[FuXi-ENS]** (arXiv 2024) — *VAE-based ML ensemble forecast achieving superior CRPS against ECMWF ENS on 98.1% of metrics.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05925) | 💻 [Code](https://zenodo.org/records/15124541)
  - 👥 Xiaohui Zhong et al.

- **[SEEDS]** (Science Advances 2024) — *Diffusion model generating NWP ensemble emulations at 1/1000th the cost of traditional ensemble runs.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14066)
  - 👥 Lizao Li et al. (Google)
