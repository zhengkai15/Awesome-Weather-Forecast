# 🏗️ Atmospheric Foundation Models

Large-scale pre-trained models supporting multiple downstream weather and climate tasks.

---

- **[ClimaX]** (ICML 2023) — *First weather and climate foundation model pre-trained on heterogeneous climate datasets for multi-task fine-tuning.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10343)
  - 👥 Tung Nguyen et al. (Microsoft Research)

- **[Aurora]** (Nature 2025) — *1.3B-parameter Microsoft atmospheric foundation model; fine-tuned variants outperform specialized models on forecasting, air quality, and extreme weather.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13063)
  - 👥 Cristian Bodnar et al. (Microsoft Research)

- **[Prithvi WxC]** (ICLR 2025 Workshop) — *IBM/NASA 2.3B-parameter foundation model trained on 160 MERRA-2 variables, supporting multi-task fine-tuning including downscaling and nowcasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13598) | 💻 [Code](https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial)
  - 👥 Mahesh Ramavajjala et al. (IBM / NASA)

- **[ESFM]** (arXiv 2025) — *Earth System Foundation Model framework unifying heterogeneous data (ERA5 / CMIP6 / satellite / station) for multi-source pre-training.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00850)
  - 👥 Jing Han et al.

- **[WIND]** (arXiv 2026) — *Unsupervised video diffusion atmospheric prior enabling zero-shot forecasting, downscaling, and assimilation without task-specific fine-tuning.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03924)
  - 👥 Jing Han et al.
