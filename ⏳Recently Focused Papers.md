## 🔥 2025

- **[Aurora]** (Nature 2025) — *1.3B-parameter Microsoft atmospheric foundation model surpassing specialized models on 5-day global forecast, air quality, and extreme weather.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13063)

- **[AlphaPre]** (CVPR 2025) — *Amplitude-phase disentanglement model for precipitation nowcasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Lin_AlphaPre_Amplitude-Phase_Disentanglement_Model_for_Precipitation_Nowcasting_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/linkenghong/AlphaPre)

- **[WoFSCast]** (GRL 2025) — *First GraphCast-based storm-warning-scale ML model for Watch-to-Warning convective guidance.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2024GL112383)

- **[Prithvi WxC]** (ICLR 2025 Workshop) — *IBM/NASA 2.3B-parameter foundation model trained on 160 MERRA-2 variables.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13598) | 💻 [Code](https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial)

- **[WLA]** (arXiv 2025.03) — *Encoding complete ERA5 (244 TB) into 0.43 TB latent dataset for efficient downstream training.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06623)

- **[ESFM]** (arXiv 2025.05) — *Earth System Foundation Model unifying ERA5/CMIP6/satellite/station heterogeneous data.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00850)

- **[Omni-Weather]** (arXiv 2025) — *First multimodal foundation model unifying radar weather generation and understanding with Chain-of-Thought reasoning.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21643)

## 🔥 2024

- **[NeuralGCM]** (Nature 2024) — *Differentiable atmospheric solver + ML parameterization; first hybrid GCM unifying weather and climate simulation.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07222) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/neuralgcm/neuralgcm)

- **[GenCast]** (Nature 2024) — *Diffusion-based probabilistic ensemble forecast outperforming ECMWF ENS on 97.2% of metrics.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15796)

- **[AIFS]** (ECMWF 2024) — *First operational GNN data-driven system from ECMWF replacing IFS deterministic forecast in production.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01465)

- **[AIFS-CRPS]** (ECMWF 2024) — *CRPS-trained ML ensemble model outperforming IFS ensemble median on 97%+ metrics.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15832)

- **[DiffDA]** (ICML 2024) — *Diffusion model for data assimilation using pre-trained GraphCast, recovering global initial fields from sparse observations.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05932) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/spcl/DiffDA)

- **[CasCast]** (ICML 2024) — *Cascaded framework decoupling mesoscale and small-scale randomness for extreme precipitation nowcasting.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04290) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/OpenEarthLab/CasCast)

- **[Stormer]** (NeurIPS 2024) — *Minimalist transformer with randomized dynamics objectives, outperforming prior methods at 7+ day lead.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03876) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/tung-nd/stormer)

- **[DiffCast]** (CVPR 2024) — *Unified diffusion framework decomposing precipitation into deterministic motion and stochastic residuals.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06734) | 💻 [Code](https://github.com/DeminYu98/DiffCast)

- **[FuXi-ENS]** (arXiv 2024) — *VAE-based ML ensemble forecast achieving superior CRPS against ECMWF ENS on 98.1% of metrics.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05925)

- **[ExtremeCast]** (arXiv 2024) — *Asymmetric Exloss function for improved global extreme weather prediction.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01295)

- **[CRA5]** (arXiv 2024) — *Compressing 226 TB ERA5 to 0.7 TB (>300:1) via VAEformer while retaining downstream forecast accuracy.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03376)

- **[SEEDS]** (Science Advances 2024) — *Diffusion model generating NWP ensemble emulations 1000× faster than traditional ensemble runs.*
  - 📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14066)
